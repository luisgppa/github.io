Series · Landscapes of War

Landscapes
of War

I take refuge in discarded material. I rescue metals, abandoned olive wood, obsolete mechanical components. I recognize in them memory and abandonment. They offer me a place to be present.

I engrave the pain that images cause me. I transfer satellite photographs and fix exact coordinates through oxidation, welding, and abrasion. I convert geographies of media violence into verifiable matter.

I don't know what legitimacy I have to build testimonies, but someone has to fix the coordinates before they erase them.
